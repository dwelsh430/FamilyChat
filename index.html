Welcome to Family Chat

Messages go straight between family members' devices. Each device keeps its own copy of the chat.

Everyone must type the same code. Treat it like a password: anyone who has it can join.

Connection relay

Phones on mobile data often can't connect straight to other devices. A relay (a "TURN server") passes the already-encrypted traffic along when that happens. It can't read your messages. Enter the details from your relay provider. Family members get them automatically when they connect, and in invite links.
